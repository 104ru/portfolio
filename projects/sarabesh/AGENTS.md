# Sarabesh visual verification

After every visual placement change, inspect the rendered scene from the affected viewing angle before presenting the change as finished. Do not rely on coordinate changes or successful loading as visual verification.

For residents, check that feet or wheels touch a clear floor surface, apparent size matches distance, and furniture correctly occludes anything behind it. Inspect nearby viewing angles as well. Never place residents on tables or stools unintentionally. Preserve approved artwork and avoid unnecessary asset generation.

If the visual result has not been inspected, explicitly say so; do not claim that the placement is correct.

NPCs are atmospheric only for this POC. Do not add NPC descriptions, hover names, dialogue cards or click popups, including for keepers or other key residents. Preserve place descriptions and map journal notes.

For panorama changes, verify all 64 lighting images across all ten scenes, including all six spherical directions and the exact images loaded in the browser. Pixel dimensions alone do not establish that the proper redraw is installed. The full-detail master must supply solid geometry in every lighting state.
