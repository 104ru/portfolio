# Instrument finish

Reference: Sandy’s Dwellers control-bezel-v2.png and bronze-material-v1.png from Sources/DwellersApp/Resources/Theme, inspected before reuse. The bezel remains in assets/ui. The bronze texture used by the old CSS switch has been removed; its replacement is one matching illustrated ON/OFF pair, with provenance in ui-switch-art.json.

Copper edge #b87532, polished brass #e4bc70, oxidized recess #202e23, dark cut #23190e, engraved ivory #f1da9f. The bezel carries the material; crisp vector symbols remain legible at control size. Georgia provides the chart’s place names; the dial uses brass pictograms with accessible names and tooltips. No floating text around persistent controls.

Map medallion and indoor toggle sit at the lower left. Time instrument sits at the lower right. Retain the scene, ten nodes, time logic, keyboard semantics and small footprint. The later request adds direct map travel, porthole previews and curved paths for the demo. The map cap and chart portholes use the actual Dwellers bezel, the lever has a recessed slot and solid pivot, and the time dial uses the same bezel with deliberate ticks, stops and a central pointer. Bulb/power marks replace awkward On/Off labels; selected time uses a pictogram inside the closed instrument. Expanded symbols sit within the ring.

Critique before implementation: extra gears, straps, plaques and decorative prose would obscure the scenery. Use one family of fasteners and material highlights, keep the rest quiet. Do not copy Dwellers navigation or enlarge this into a dashboard.
