# Production panorama redraw

The ten approved locations retain their composition and handcrafted steampunk direction. Built-in image generation redrew registered overlapping views rather than repeatedly enlarging the old soft panorama. Six views cover each sphere, including its ceiling/sky and floor/ground. They are projected and feathered into a 6144 × 3072 equirectangular texture. The first landing proof used the previously approved viewport as its front view.

Lighting states now use the full-detail assembled master as their actual solid geometry and material artwork. The earlier method enlarged a small generated lighting image and added only a fine-detail residual; that lost medium-scale drawn construction and made several variants look unprocessed. The corrected pipeline uses those existing lighting images only to derive illumination fields, then applies the fields to the detailed master. Large sky regions retain the selected weather; small blue books and painted metal are excluded from that sky selection. The balcony opening was corrected in one image edit to reveal the actual bedroom from outside.

All 64 production states remain 6144 × 3072. Nine unchanged detailed master states remain WebP; 55 rebuilt states use high-quality AVIF with full chroma detail, preserving image dimensions while keeping the complete deployment below its archive limit. There is one selected production file per state. No original scene is replaced by a low-resolution lighting export. The generator did not export native 6K lighting images; the production panoramas are assembled textures.

## Prompt set

View redraw (style-transfer): Keep the supplied registered 110° camera view and every doorway, window, furnishing and boundary. Redraw coherent fine pipes, rivets, worn brass, teal paint, timber, fabrics and glass in the approved handcrafted painterly steampunk style. Keep the current lighting. No new objects, people, text or interface. Up and down views preserve their exact camera orientation and continue the architecture or ground across the pole.

Lighting (lighting-weather): Change only the exact complete sphere’s lighting to morning, day, evening or night, with the existing electrical fixtures on or off. Preserve camera, geometry and object positions. Morning is softly golden; day has a clear blue sky; evening is peach-violet dusk; night is deep blue with stars and distant amber windows. Off means unlit bulbs and no artificial amber light pools, while retaining enough natural ambient visibility for navigation. Windowless rooms have only light-on and light-off states. Repair doubled seam edges without redesign. No added objects, text or interface; complete 2:1 sphere with painted poles.

The balcony’s electrical state follows the apartment lights visible through its window. Exterior time remains independent. The landing follows the rocket interior lighting visible through its window.

No exploration takes were requested. Additional corrections addressed observed town join/pole defects and daylight star contamination. Working view guides, native lighting exports and assembly previews are removed after the final set is installed and checked. Production retains one selected texture per state.

## Validation

The asset verification report records decoding, dimensions, painted pole checks and a checksum for every selected state. Browser review checks the panorama projection, map travel, time dial and interior switch. High-resolution assembly improves close-view detail but does not make this a 3D geometry scene or introduce continuous walking.
