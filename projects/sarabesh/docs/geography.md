# V1 geography — ten viewpoints

## Arrival and narrative

A short illustrated rocket landing introduces Sarabesh, followed by a large city title and a clear exploration-only POC invitation. Begin inside the rocket exit room; its viewport shows the landing ground and city beyond. The Last Electrician will eventually restore the water station and bring the desert into bloom. This ten-scene POC establishes the world without quest interactions, inventory or puzzle gates.

## Geography

The rocket and landing ground sit on the town’s approach. The town remains the central hub, with four existing building entrances. Cogswash Residence contains a ground-floor foyer and kitchen, upstairs living quarters, and its existing outward-facing valley viewpoint. There is no travel from the valley viewpoint into a second region.

Each connection works in both directions. No one-way doors, disconnected nodes or puzzle locks are introduced.

Sandy dropped the Paris idea during visual production. No Paris assets, map entry or travel route are part of V1.

## Lighting classification

Rocket exit room, living quarters, kitchen, grocery and tavern have daylight through windows or glazing. Foyer and motel are windowless. Every interior has one independently switched artificial light. Daylight remains visible with the light off.

The landing-ground texture follows the rocket lamp state visible through its viewport. The valley-view texture follows the apartment lamp state visible through its window. These are extra lighting states, not extra viewpoints or exterior switches.

## Completed POC

The ten viewpoints have 64 selected lighting textures. The current browser experience is at the repository root; the unchanged eight-scene tour is retained under `viewer/` as source provenance. No objects, puzzle gates, scene animations or audio were added. Extra doors and stairs remain scenery for later rooms.
