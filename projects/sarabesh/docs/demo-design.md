# Sarabesh demo direction

Keep the existing hand-painted world and illustrated Dwellers instruments. Use deep jade #19372d, night iron #101c26, worn brass #b98543, pale brass #e2bf70, ivory #f0deb5 and desert ochre #b67845. Rye carries the large city title and place names; Georgia carries readable narrative copy. The memorable opening is a single illustrated rocket landing, followed by a left-aligned brass-framed invitation with the city visible behind it. No headers, footers or dashboard cards.

The title is large; the POC and exploration-only scope is explicit. Intro motion is skippable and reduced motion goes straight to the invitation. Exploration retains the tiny illustrated controls, with a soft lower scrim for contrast. First-visit notes use one small brass-framed journal card. The map keeps curved routes and picture portholes: visited views are fully visible; unvisited views stay shadowed even on hover. Only visited places expose a separate brass information button. All ten locations remain available for map travel.

This follows the approved material style rather than a generic landing-page layout. Existing panorama geometry, times, lighting and carefully aligned travel points remain intact.
