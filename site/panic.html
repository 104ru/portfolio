No Grounds for Panic

Alex Kartashov and Sandy Tayts

I co-wrote these science-fiction stories with Alex Kartashov. I developed the illustrations with Midjourney, then combined and refined the images in Photoshop. Claude helped with translation and layout.

Fine lines, dots, and layered scenes give very different stories a shared black-and-white world.
