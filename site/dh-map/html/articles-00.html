VPN Connection

When to connect to the EY network through VPN, and when it is better to leave it off.

4.8out of 5

Current State: Secure VPN Connection

A virtual private network (VPN) creates an encrypted tunnel between your device and the EY network. Use it whenever you work outside an EY office and need internal resources: file shares, intranet sites, on-premise applications or a remote desktop. Traffic inside the tunnel cannot be read on the public Wi-Fi you are connected to.

Connect before you open the application you need, not after. If the VPN client reports a certificate or time-out error, check your internet connection first, then restart the client. Most connection problems are solved by signing out and back in once.

When VPN is Not an Answer

Cloud services such as Microsoft 365, Teams and SharePoint Online are reachable without VPN and are faster without it. Routing them through the tunnel adds latency and takes bandwidth from colleagues who need it for internal systems.

VPN also does not fix a slow home connection, a locked account or a device that is out of compliance. For those, use Reset Password, My Device is Not Compliant or Raise an Issue from the Home screen.
