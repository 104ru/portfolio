Size Chart

Size

Most of our footwear is true to size. Measure heel to longest toe on a hard surface, standing with your weight on the foot. If you're between two lengths, round up to the next size. We build shoes from Size 6 to size 16 in half-size increments. Beefroll series are reported to be slightly roomier, we recommend to order 1/2 size smaller for a tighter fit.

Width

We use the standard US width letter system. We make four widths: B (narrow), D (medium/standard), E (wide), and EE / 2E (extra wide). D is the standard medium width; B is narrow, and E and EE run progressively wider.
