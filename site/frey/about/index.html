Our Story

In a small town in Maine, a group of residents, passionate about quality eyewear, decided to start their own brand. Inspired by one founder's beloved French Bulldog, Frenchie, they named their venture "French-Eye". Their mission: to create premium, locally-made glasses, combining style with exceptional craftsmanship.

FRench + EYe = FREY

"French-Eye" quickly gained popularity for its unique blend of local artisanship and chic design. The founders, committed to quality, sourced materials from the best suppliers, ensuring that each pair of glasses not only looked great but stood the test of time. Frenchie, the dog, became the charming mascot, embodying the brand's friendly and approachable spirit.

Embracing their roots, "French-Eye" became a symbol of Maine's dedication to quality and community. Their eyewear, reflecting the rugged beauty of their surroundings, offered a personal touch in an industry dominated by mass production, making "French-Eye" a beloved local treasure.

Aby Johnson, the creative force behind French-Eye, blends innovative design with sustainability, crafting eyewear that's both stylish and environmentally conscious.
