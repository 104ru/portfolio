Alternative Approaches
Propose multiple methods to achieve a task, ensuring flexibility in problem-solving.
"I have this boy whom i love so much. Propose 3 strategies how to make him notice me and like me"
Certainly, here are three strategies to make him notice you and potentially develop feelings for you:
Be Authentic and Engaging: Show your true self and engage in conversations about topics that genuinely interest both of you. By being authentic, you'll create a connection that goes beyond superficial interactions and make him more likely to notice your uniqueness.
Friendship and Shared Activities: Build a foundation of friendship by participating in activities you both enjoy. Spend time together in group settings or one-on-one, engaging in shared hobbies or interests. Friendships often evolve into deeper relationships, allowing him to get to know you better.
Positive Vibes and Compliments: Surround yourself with positivity and exude confidence. Offer sincere compliments to boost his self-esteem and show your appreciation for his qualities. Positive interactions can leave a lasting impression and make him more receptive to forming a closer bond.
