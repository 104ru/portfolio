CRM Admin App
Our partnership with Microsoft resulted in the creation of an omnichannel customer engagement experience tailored for MS Dynamics CRM solutions. This comprehensive solution is composed of two key components:
​
​
​Omnichannel
Chat for Seamless Communication:
Our team designed and developed a CRM chat feature that seamlessly integrates with MS Dynamics CRM. This empowers businesses to engage with customers across various communication channels, enhancing the overall customer experience.
​
Admin Portal for Effortless Management
: We conceptualized, researched, designed, and implemented an Admin portal. This portal equips company administrators with the tools needed to efficiently manage user permissions, installation processes, and ongoing maintenance. It streamlines the system and ensures a smooth operational environment.
