Storytelling Demos
Storytelling animations get an idea across and  keep on working: you can share them to as many stakeholders as you like.
The following storytelling demos were used for stakeholder engagement and development team alignment. If you end up watching one and think, "Hey, that was pretty straightforward," then I've done my job right.
The Humana Onboarding Transformation animation was used to  align the cross-functional team. Made on the base of  in-depth process research, it outlines the existing onboarding procedure, pinpoints its flaws and shows the critical need for transformation. It also presents the solution to a challenge from a strategic, somewhat sales-oriented perspective. This animation aims to  instill a sense of urgency for upcoming change.
The
Day in Life of Ford Mobile Service
is designed for a presale presentation. I is focused  on the implementation of a mobile service system, through  "day in the life" scenario for attending mobile appointments.  Straightforward animation makes and impression of a seamless and efficient  service

The
HCSC Day in Life
video is a classic "Art of Possible" sales pitch, designed to ignite the client's imagination. As a key component of the co-creation HCSC workshop, this animation aims to expand the horizons of what can be achieved, encouraging stakeholders to think beyond the conventional and co-create innovative solutions together.
The
Credit Check In One Minute
serves as a foundational tool for initiating discussions by aligning stakeholders on the intricacies of the sales process. It is routinely circulated and consulted whenever a decision is on the horizon, ensuring everyone involved has a clear understanding of the process at hand.
