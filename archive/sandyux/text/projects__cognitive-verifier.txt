Cognitive Verifier
Have LLM generate sub-questions that refine the user's main question, making answers more tailored.
"I'm planning a holiday in Europe. What do you need to know to help me best?"
"Destination Preferences: Which specific countries or cities in Europe are you interested in visiting?
Travel Dates: When do you plan to travel? Provide exact dates or a general time frame.
Travel Companions: Are you traveling alone, with family, friends, or a partner? How many people are in your group?
Interests and Activities: What types of activities do you enjoy? Are you looking for cultural experiences, outdoor adventures, historical sites, shopping, or relaxation?
Budget: What is your estimated budget for the trip, including accommodations, transportation, activities, and meals?
Accommodation Preferences: Do you prefer hotels, hostels, vacation rentals, or other types of accommodations??
