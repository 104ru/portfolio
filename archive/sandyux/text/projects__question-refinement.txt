Question Refinement
Instruct LLM to refine or suggest improvements to user questions, making information retrieval more precise.
"Refine my questions as follows: "need more money" -> "How can I adopt a more successful financial strategy"
"I want a dog"
"How do I write a resume"
"When to go to doctor"
"Why do anything"
"Do you love me"
"How to get a boy to love me"
"New fashion in this summer"
"I need more money" -> "How can I improve my financial situation?"
"I want a dog" -> "What steps should I take to responsibly adopt a dog?"
"How do I write a resume" -> "What are the key elements of creating an effective resume?"
"When to go to doctor" -> "What are the indicators for when to seek medical attention?"
"Why do anything" -> "What are the motivations behind taking action?"
"Do you love me" -> "How can I build stronger emotional connections?"
"How to get a boy to love me" -> "What approaches foster meaningful relationships?"
"New fashion in this summer" -> "What are the latest summer fashion trends?"
