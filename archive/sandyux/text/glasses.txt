FREY Glasses Branding and UX /UI Design
Client Brief
FREY is a sunglasses brand in Portland, Maine. We wanted it to speak in vivid colors and bold shapes, embracing a playful yet sophisticated aesthetic. Design language combines mint and peach hues with the clean, modernist typography of Chivo and Inria, creating a visual image that's as fresh and uplifting.

Desktop Wireframes and Prototyping
The client is focused on connecting with their customers through a variety of platforms. Leveraging the desktop channel, we can create a more visually engaging experience. Our initial step involves developing desktop wireframes, which will be closely aligned with the mobile wireframes, ensuring consistency in logic and components across both platforms.
