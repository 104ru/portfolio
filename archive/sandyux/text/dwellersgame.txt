DWELLERS
Scavenger hunt in augmented reality space
​
The game is based around a dieselpunk-style augmented reality. Using mobile devices, gamers will search for and take photos of the D
wellers (small mechanical creatures), which
tend to hang out
around tourist attractions,
thickly settled areas,
shopping malls
and in other places with heavy foot traffic.
The super goal of the game is to provide the platform to show the beauty and wonder in the everyday environment. World is full of strange and wonderful creatures, and now everyone is able to see them
Game Modes and Gamer Profiles
Hunting
Gamers hunt Dwellers in a photo safari. If you find one on the map, you come close, open your camera and see the creature in the augmented reality of the app.
You take a picture of the creature with your phone.  You might want to add some filters/frames to the photo. Then you can the dweller's portrait in the Dweller photo feed or Instagram. You get likes. Likes are points in Dwellers' universe. You can use the points to brag, be proud of it, compete, and get rewards.
​
Hunting is the default mode of the Dwellers game.
Hunters: Default User Group
Hunters are gamers who are mostly interested in chasing, photographing, and sharing the dweller images. Their other interests may include photography, travel, design, fitness, fitness applications, AR games, Instagram, social games.
​
Hunters: Business Value
Hunters work like an advertising tool. They create the traffic to the Dwellers page on Social media, popularize the game, and ignite interest.
Expensive hobbies and interest assumptions like travel and photography allow for targeted sales ads for this group of users.
​
​
​
Hunters: Gamer Profile
Hunters care most about action and achievement: their goal is to create the most compelling image with the most likes. Most of the hunters are very competitive and social.
​
​
​
​
Building
Where did the Dwellers come from? Some of them are generated by the system, but most are build by gamers inside the Builder part of the game.
Building a Dweller is a simple construction game. When building, you can use the standard and premium details for your Dweller. After the Dweller is built, you can release it into the wild: either nearby (standard mode), or near in high-traffic areas (premium mode).
After your Dweller is released, each time it is captured, published, and liked, you receive points. You can use these points to compete with other builders or buy the premium parts/releases.
​
Building is supposed to be the most revenue-generating actibvity inside the game
Builders: Content-Generating User Group
Gamers mostly create and release the dwellers. Their goal is to make the most compelling/interesting creature and get the most hits from the hunters' photos. Other interests may include design, steampunk/dieselpunk, and similar post-modern phenomena, construction, puzzles, sims and other non-violent games.
​
Builders: Business Value
Builders provide custom content to the game and make the Dweller universe truly unique. They will be the most active participants in in-game purchases.
They can be the target audience for design-related goods and services. They also might be responsible for promoting the game in more "grown-up" ways, like blogs and articles.
​
​
Builders: Gamer Profile
Builders
are invested in the creative and strategical aspects of the game, as well as social competition and community.

Social Monitoring
Dwellers platform is highly dependent on social media activity. After the dweller was built, released, photographed, and published, both builder and hunter expect some appreciation. Social approval (likes comments and shares) is a third component of the Dwellers game, and it is equally important as the other two.
​
Social Monitoring brings the game to life and acts as a promotion mechanism. It is also definitely an engagement path into the active participation in the game, even in its a most passive form (just watching)
Monitors: Social Base for the Game
Monitors mostly watch dwellers' feeds and rate/like/share/comment on the photographs.
Interests may include social interactions, steampunk/dieselpunk, and other post-modern culture, animation, travel photos, and
The main interest in this group is Immersion into the dweller universe and social community interaction.
​
Monitors: Business Value
Monitors' value is indirect. They don't bring revenue as such but make the Dweller world real. The more social buzz is generated, the more likes and shares they produce, the more motivation the hunters and builders get to build and expand their world. Monitor users also could be the natural source for expanding the active user base
