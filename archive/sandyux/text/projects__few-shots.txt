Few Shots
Giving LLM few use cases in a certain format, then giving the input. LLM will process it in the same format. It yields great results when used with fact checking and tail generation.
Give me 3 of those:
Adventure: Lost in a dense jungle
Prompt: Explore the mysterious jungle as you search for a way back to civilization, encountering hidden creatures and ancient ruins.
Adventure: Journey to a distant planet
Prompt: Embark on a perilous space mission to an unknown planet, where alien landscapes and unexpected challenges await.
"Adventure: Climbing the Snow-Capped Peak
Prompt: Battle against fierce winds and freezing temperatures as you ascend a mysterious mountain, seeking the ancient temple rumored to be at its pinnacle.
Adventure: Delving into the Abyssal Depths
Prompt: Dive into the darkest parts of the ocean, where strange creatures and glowing plants guide your path to the lost city submerged for millennia.
Adventure: Roaming the Enchanted Forest
Prompt: Wander through a mystical forest where trees whisper secrets, magical creatures guide or mislead, and a legendary artifact waits to be discovered."
