Chain of Thought
Asking LLM to provide reasoning for the answer in a certain format. Can be very useful in defining results and minimizing hallucinations/lies.
"I want to forage blueberries in Alaska in December. Tell me if it is good idea, and give me reasoning"
"Foraging for blueberries in Alaska in December isn't advisable. Here's why:
1. Seasonality: Blueberries ripen from late June to mid-August. By December, they're gone due to prior harvesting or natural fall.
2. Climate: December in Alaska is characterized by freezing temperatures and heavy snowfall, making foraging challenging and potentially dangerous.
3. Daylight Hours: Alaska has extremely limited daylight in December. Some northern parts even experience continuous darkness.
Conclusion: For a fruitful blueberry foraging in Alaska, plan for the summer months when conditions are more favorable"
