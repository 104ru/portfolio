Flipped Interaction
Shift the onus to LLM to ask questions, making user interaction dynamic.
in this case I am showing only the Chat questions for brevity sake
"Help me choose a book genre. Start by asking about my reading preferences."
"Alright, let's begin:"
Do you prefer fiction or non-fiction?
1. Fiction
2. Non-fiction
Would you like to read a classic novel or something contemporary?",
1. Classic
2. Contemporary
Do you prefer stories set in a realistic world or ones with fantastical elements?
1. Realistic
2. Fantastic
Do you want to dive into a world with magic and wizards or one with mythical creatures and other worlds?",
1. Magic and Wizards
2. Mythical Creatures
Alright, blending the two can be fun!
Which setting intrigues you more?
1.  A modern city where the magical world hides in plain sight.
2. A completely different world or realm where all these elements coexist."
Based on your preferences, I recommend \"The City We Became\" by N.K. Jemisin. It's a contemporary fantasy novel set in New York City where the city itself comes alive through individual avatars for its different boroughs. It combines elements of urban fantasy, magic, and a touch of Lovecraftian horror, all while commenting on the cultural heartbeat of NYC."
