Mobile Provider Buy In and Onboarding
Dish Mobile was rolling out an MVNO (Mobile Virtual Network Operator) plan that is simple and based on phone leasing. Their goal is to deliver an affordable, transparently priced service without unnecessary extras, specifically designed to appeal to the single urban dwellers aged 25 to 35
Clickable Prototype
Discovery sessions with the client resulted in the following list of requirements:
Onboarding should be very straightforward.
No outside devices allowed
Devices are leased, not sold
Simple plan structure
Transparent pricing, no hidden fees
Don't be too serious
​
